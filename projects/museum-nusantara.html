Museum Nusantara

Three.js · WebGL 3D virtual gallery

An interactive 3D virtual museum web app with first-person controls, showcasing Nusantara cultural exhibits (Reog dance, Gandrung, and more) complete with lighting and informative UI overlays. 3D assets sourced and curated from Sketchfab under Creative Commons licensing

Highlights

  • First-person controls (WASD & mouse look) with physics and jump mechanics
  • Spatial raycasting for object interaction and visual highlighting
  • Collision detection system and ambient / spatial audio

Tech stack

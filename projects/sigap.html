SIGAP

Flutter · Team of 3 Android

A mobile app combining real-time BMKG data, a Gemini 2.5 Flash AI chatbot, and community-based reporting to help people cope with natural disasters. Supports SDG 11, SDG 3, and SDG 13.

Features

Real-time BMKG data

Earthquakes & weather for 7 major cities.

SIGAP AI Chatbot

Multi-turn, topic-locked to disaster guidance.

Emergency SOS

Sends GPS location, monitored by admins.

Proximity alert

Notifications for disasters near your location.

Interactive map

Admin-verified markers via OpenStreetMap.

Community reporting

Photo & location reports, admin-verified.

Tech stack

Flutter / Dart Firebase Auth & Firestore Gemini AI API BMKG API OpenStreetMap
